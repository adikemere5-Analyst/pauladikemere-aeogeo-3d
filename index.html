Before vs. After: Why AI Skips Generic Copy

AI answer engines favor direct, self-contained facts over vague marketing slogans. Here is how we restructure a service page section.

BEFORE — SKIPPED BY AI ENGINES
"Welcome to our premier remodeling company! With years of dedication and quality craftsmanship, we turn your dream home into reality. Contact us today for a consultation."
  • ✗ No city/neighborhood service-area specificity
  • ✗ No typical project cost ranges, timelines, or permitting facts
  • ✗ Missing license verification and structured Q&A headings
AFTER — ENGINEERED FOR AI CITATION
"How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Woodland Hills, CA? A licensed full kitchen remodel in Woodland Hills typically ranges from $45,000 to $95,000+ and takes 6–10 weeks including LA City permitting, structural updates, and cabinetry."
  • ✓ Question heading matches natural buyer prompts
  • ✓ First sentence gives a direct, quotable answer with real numbers
  • ✓ Backed by matching LocalBusiness & FAQPage JSON-LD schema
